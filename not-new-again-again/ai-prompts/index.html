an idea for how to make the ai a better companion

i realised that i'm asking better questions as the AI is getting more capable.
Some of my recent prompts:

  1. Look at this photo of metro commuters, analyse the human behavior aspects like an experienced startup founder and give me some not-so-obvious insights about human needs / startup ideas.

  2. Why are many streaming platforms like amazon video and jio hotstar charging money to remove ads even for paid users?

  3. A potential client has asked me to give a branding proposal for his new Samosa Indian fast food chain. Based on the location pin on google map shared, analyse the kind of people / potential customers for the samosa & chat shop, the kind of people - demographics - may be around this location and who might be interested to eat here. Based on this analysis, give me a high levle brand design brief that can appeal to the target audiance.

  4. Help me write a terminal command to insert an audio clip at a certain timestamp in the video on my local mac

  5. these are details of 2 water heaters. i want to decide whether i should replace the old one with the new one. my criteria are speed of heating water & energy efficiency. cost has no bearing as i've already bought the recold one from my old house. my values are - low carbon footprint, using a product to its full working life. wasting less resources, experiencing convinience when bathing. the old one is 25 yr old and may need repairing.
    I think i've upped my prompting game because i've improved the quality of my questions.

idea even though the ai is the same, we can help users improve what they use it for. what if when you ask an ai a question/prompt, the ai gives you a few options based on the values you set. e.g. if you set 'curiosity, learning, improving quality of thinking, relationships, etc. as your important values' the ai can then give you. a few options to select

e.g. you ask "explain the concept of ad based business model"
it asks you " would you rather know more about:

  1. What are some adjecent business models related to ads

  2. how do advertisers decide the cost of an ad?

  3. select most likely options to dig deeper > a. b.c.

  4. A non obvious but more interesting question could be ....

like a great teacher, you dont just give answers, you help people arrive at their own answers by channeling your curiosity. and gives multiple paths to follow.
